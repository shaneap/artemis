## Turn gaps and what faster turning is worth

A turn gap is the idle time on a table between one stay ending (payment) and the next starting, within a night. Under the old count a table borrowed by a combined party looked idle, so its gaps ran straight through people sitting at it. Room fullness is the slot the gap began in.
