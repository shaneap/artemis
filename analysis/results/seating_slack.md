# Friday and Saturday evenings: could one more party have been seated?

147 Friday and Saturday nights, Apr 25, 2025 to Sep 26, 2026; arrivals every 15 minutes from 6pm to 9:45pm. The new party stays the typical weekend-evening time for its size (2: 86 min, 4: 102 min, 6: 116 min). Nobody who actually came is moved in time or turned away.

- **As seated**: a table or listed combination was free for the whole stay, as the floor was actually used.
- **Re-seated**: the same parties given different tables in their own section could have made room.
- **Any section**: the same, letting parties move between sections.
- **Full**: not even that. Anyone else wanting a table then was turned away or waited; Toast can't see them.

## Reading it

- Re-seating recovers a slot that the floor as used had blocked in 2% of blocked party-of-6 cases (2: 53%, 4: 23%). That is capacity lost to which table each party got, not to having too few tables.
- The **Full** column is where seating policy can't help: more tables were wanted than existed. How many parties were turned away in those slots, and whether people booked elsewhere, needs reservation data (Tock): requests, denials, waitlists and no-shows.
